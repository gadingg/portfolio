# After audit: video CMS

Mode after, session override. Existing gallery cards, titles, borders, controls and palette are reused. No new card design or decoration. Admin makes video upload and thumbnail actions discoverable with short labels, progress, recovery and existing save/publish states. Native video controls handle sound, seeking and pause. Thumbnail failure preserves the uploaded file and prompts a manual cover; publication rejects an uploaded video with no cover. Public playback errors retain the poster and a clickable fallback. At most one preview plays, and reduced motion leaves it static.

Scoped layout detector returned no findings. Public desktop and 390px browser fixtures demonstrate one active muted preview, no page overflow, modal isolation and restored focus. Automatic cover capture produced a 960x536 JPEG. This is functional and layout evidence, not a claim that an Instagram embed can autoplay. Production publication remains untouched.
