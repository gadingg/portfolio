Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of problems do you typically solve?

I bridge marketing and operations. If your team is stuck with repetitive manual tasks, needs a custom listing hub or reporting tool, or wants higher-performing Meta Ads and campaign concepts, I handle both the creative concept and the technical build.

How do you combine marketing, design, and web development?

Rather than handing off designs to an external team, I build the solutions directly using modern web technologies like Next.js and TailwindCSS. This eliminates communication gaps and ensures the final tool matches the marketing intent.

How fast does a typical campaign or web-app take?

Focused campaign concepts and visual sprints run in one to two weeks. Custom internal web applications, dashboards, or gamification tools typically take two to four weeks from concept to deployment.
