## Limits

The local environment has no `ADMIN_EMAIL`, so real authenticated admin login and live storage writes were not exercised. Configure the existing authorized Supabase user's email before local admin use; authentication was not relaxed. Public storage is not private draft storage. Revision checking catches ordinary stale edits but cannot provide transactional compare-and-swap across simultaneous Storage writes; use one editor session.

Dummy video entries do not contain someone else's Instagram posts. Replace them in Works with public, embeddable posts and thumbnails. Instagram owns the embedded player and may require a further Play tap; private, removed or restricted posts use the external-link fallback. Physical devices, screen readers and deployed hosting have not been verified in this pass.
