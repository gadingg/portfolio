Frequently Asked Questions

How do you usually work with clients?

We start with an alignment call, identify core business bottlenecks, then agree on a focused scope covering design, marketing, and tech builds with clear weekly milestones.

How fast can a project move?

Focused branding sprints or campaign setups run in one to three weeks. Full design systems and web application builds typically take four to eight weeks.

Can you integrate into an existing team?

Yes. I collaborate smoothly with founders, marketing heads, and engineering teams, delivering clean documentation, design tokens, and modular components.
